About ODPL

Built On Control,
Not Dependence.

Omendra Developers Pvt. Ltd. is a Delhi NCR-based interior fit-out execution company offering civil construction, painting and waterproofing services across North India. We execute to architect and designer specifications — our core strength is an in-house team of engineers spanning civil, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, networking and finishing, meaning tighter control over quality, timelines and cost, without over-dependence on external vendors. Every project is tracked end-to-end on our own in-house Project Management System (PMS) — daily site reports, material logs and digital sign-offs, in real time.
